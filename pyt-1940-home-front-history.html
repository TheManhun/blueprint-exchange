2.0A LITTLE SOMETHING FOR BOBBY THUMBSUP

👍 After the Great Depression, Bobby ThumbsUp finally found himself a steady job collecting tolls.

He thought a new job might cheer him up.

Unfortunately, his first performance review came back with just 67% approval.

Poor Bobby. He turns up every day, sits behind his little glass window and does his best.

If you're enjoying 1940 Home Front, give poor Bobby a thumbs-up. You might just make his day. 👍

  • In plain terms: the 1940 huts now have see-through windows, a furnished interior — and Bobby and his colleagues at work inside
  • Left-hand traffic: with the Left Hand Traffic mod, the whole 1940 plaza is built mirrored for left-hand traffic — islands, huts and the split before them — with every board still reading the right way round, right the moment you place it
  • With Pay Your Tolls: one toll keeper per hut — a full crew earns +25% on the takings; a bigger plaza may need a minute to fill up after the update
  • Your existing plazas update themselves: no bulldozing
1.1THE TRAMS ARE BACK

Rationing is over: the tramlines have been dug back up out of the victory gardens, and the trams run straight through the plaza again.

  • In plain terms: on a road with tram track, the plaza lays its own — along the kerb lane and through the BUSES & TRUCKS booth
  • The bus and truck lane is now at the kerb: 3 lanes are CARS | CARS | BUSES & TRUCKS, 2 lanes CARS | BUSES & TRUCKS. Re-place a plaza to get the new lanes
  • Cleaner two-way ends — each direction leaves your road straight, no more pinched waist
  • On a country road the plaza’s ends become your road — same width and markings
  • A plaza you’ve just placed sets itself up at once — its ends, then its track
  • 1940 Home Front in 12 languages — every language of the game
  • Very old 1940 plazas convert themselves to today’s plaza, keeping their settings
  • With Harbour City installed too, both always run the newest plaza
1.0.3LANES AND WHOLE-ROAD BARRIERS
  • Buses and trucks get their own lane through the 1940 plaza: 3 lanes are cars | buses & trucks | cars, 2 lanes buses & trucks | cars. Re-place a plaza to get the new lanes
  • One Barriers button with every style, the sandbag wall included, and a new length, Whole road: the wall runs the whole stretch, round the curves, and stops at junctions
  • The drawn sandbag wall has left the menus (it ran on into junctions). Any you've already built stay where they are
  • With Pay Your Tolls 2.6.2: your plaza's two toll keepers now count — up to +25% takings and maintenance for your buses and trucks within 300 m

Blueprint Exchange and the PYT mods are fan-made community projects for Transport Fever 2 and Transport Fever 3 players. It is unofficial and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or the responsibility of the game’s developers or publishers.

Inspired by Copy It! by okeating — a superb TF2 utility whose approach to capturing and rebuilding constructions and track is the foundation Blueprint Exchange builds on.
