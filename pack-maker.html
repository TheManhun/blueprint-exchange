      2. Try it. Start Transport Fever 3 and load a game with PYT - Blueprint Exchange and your pack switched on. In the BPX Library pick Show: Packs and paste one.
      3. Upload it from the game to mod.io as you would any mod — run its Validation Report first.
      4. Keep the folder. The game remembers your mod.io page in it: to update the pack, change the files there and upload again.

      Help

      Why make a pack?

      A blueprint file can be shared by hand, but only on a PC, and the other player has to find the right folder. A pack is a mod: players subscribe to it on mod.io like any other, and its blueprints show up in their BPX Library on their own.

      Is anything uploaded when I use this page?

      No. Your files are read, checked and zipped right here in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere — you upload the pack yourself, from the game.

      Why was one of my files refused?

      The Pack Maker only takes BPX blueprint files, and only plain data: track, roads, stations, signals and their settings. Anything else in a file (like a piece of program code) is refused, so a pack can never carry something unexpected to the people who subscribe.

      My blueprint uses another mod’s track or roads

      Then players need that mod too. Say so in “More about it”, so it’s on the pack’s mod.io page.
