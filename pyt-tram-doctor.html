PYT - Tram Doctor

Every tram line checked in one window -- stops, track and wires -- and fixed in a click. Turning a bus line into trams? Plan it, lay the track, buy enough trams, retire the buses.

What it does

Every tram line on your map checked in one window: are the stops on the tram lane, is the track joined up, are the wires there? Fix what’s wrong with a click — or fly straight to it.

Trams are the fiddliest thing in the game to set up. You lay the track, place the stops, buy the trams — and they sit in the depot, turn round halfway, or drive a lap of the town to reach a stop across the street. Tram Doctor follows every line the way a tram drives it, tells you what’s wrong in plain words, and fixes what it can.

What you get

  • Stops — a stop on a wide street has a bay per lane, and trams can only use the ones on the tram lanes. A line set to the wrong bay, or to the wrong side of the road, is found — and Fix the stop switches it
  • Track — every stretch from stop to stop followed the way a tram drives it. A missing piece, or a junction where the tracks don’t join, is pointed out, and Lay tram track or Join the tracks fixes it
  • Power — electric trams need wires all the way. Unwired pieces are counted and Wire puts the wires up
  • Turning round — a tram can’t U-turn in the street. A line that needs to is caught, with the ways out: a turning station, track round the block, Split into two lines, or Turn at a station that is already on the line. A loop with no station to turn in? Make 2 tram lines, one each way — nothing ever turns round, and the old buses are sold and their line retired
  • The long way round — stops on the wrong side send trams on a lap round the block. The doctor plans the whole line and tells you what it costs
  • The game’s own verdict — what the game’s line check says, with the exact spot
  • Make the reverse line — the same stops the other way round, with a tram (or a bus) to start it — on any tram or bus line
  • Tidy the line — a line that zig-zags, or visits the same stop again and again, gets a clean round: each stop once, once round the town, without driving the same street twice
  • Electrify the tram track — wires on every piece your trams drive, whenever you’re ready for electric trams
  • Houses in the way? — the tram street is a little wider; Lay it anyway clears them, as the road tool does (your town may grumble)
  • The right trams — Create tram line buys trams that can run on your track: electric ones only when the wires are up
  • Your town first — the lines of the town the camera is on come to the top of the list
  • Bus lines too — every bus stop checked for the side of the road its buses drive on
  • From buses to trams — plan a bus line for trams (the → tram button) and see what it would need. Lay tram track along the route in one go, Create tram line with enough trams to seat at least what the buses did, then Retire the bus line when you’re ready (its buses are sold). Street mods that bring their own tram streets are used too
  • Show me — click any problem and the camera flies there
  • In your language — the window speaks your language: English plus 14 more, the same as the PYT Language setting

How to start

  1. Click the PYT button in the top bar, then Tram Doctor (the tram with the first-aid badge)
  2. Your tram lines are listed on the left, your bus lines below. Pick one: what’s wrong shows on the right
  3. Press the fix — or click the problem to fly there and fix it yourself. Nothing changes until you press a button

Mod developer’s tip: save before the big ones — laying track along a whole route or retiring a bus line changes a lot at once.

Worum es geht: Tram Doctor prüft jede Straßenbahnlinie so, wie eine Straßenbahn sie fährt – Halte, Gleis und Oberleitung – und behebt, was es kann, mit einem Klick.

  • Halte: falsche Bucht oder falsche Straßenseite wird gefunden und die Linie umgestellt
  • Gleis und Oberleitung: fehlende Stücke und Kreuzungen, an denen die Gleise nicht verbunden sind – verlegen, verbinden, verkabeln
  • Wenden: Straßenbahnen können auf der Straße nicht wenden – Wendestation, Gleis um den Block, Linie teilen oder in einer Station wenden. Ein Ring ohne Station: 2 Straßenbahnlinien, eine je Richtung
  • Vom Bus zur Straßenbahn: Buslinie planen, Gleis entlang der Strecke verlegen, Straßenbahnlinie mit genug Plätzen anlegen, Buslinie stilllegen (die Busse werden verkauft)
  • Linie aufräumen: jede Haltestelle einmal, einmal um die Stadt – ohne dieselbe Straße zweimal zu fahren
  • Straßenbahngleis elektrifizieren, Gegenlinie für jede Bahn- und Buslinie, Häuser im Weg auf Wunsch abreißen
  • Die Linien der Stadt, über der die Kamera steht, stehen oben in der Liste
  • Ein Klick auf ein Problem fliegt die Kamera hin. Das Fenster ist auf Deutsch
