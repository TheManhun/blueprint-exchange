What it does

A locked store for every cargo the factory uses and makes, a line to the factory and the trucks to run it — set up for you the moment you place it. 1,000, 2,000 or 5,000 per cargo.

PYT - Factory Buffer. Protect Your Throughput. A PYT (Play Your Transport) mod by Epod — the little brother of PYT - Mega Warehouse.

You know the moment. The livestock farm fills up with wool nobody collects, and the meat stops with it. Or the farm has spread so far your trains can't reach it any more. Factory Buffer is the small yard you drop beside the factory: it reads what the factory uses and makes, builds one locked store for each, and keeps the goods moving.

What you get

  • It reads the factory — every cargo it uses and makes gets its own locked store, the booster included (tools, machines, fertilizer). A farm's wool can never block its meat again
  • It sets up the transport — a line between the factory and the buffer, with two general trucks from its own garage. Or just the line, or just the stores: you choose when you place it
  • Store size — 1,000, 2,000 or 5,000 per cargo, and the buildings grow with it
  • Drive-through truck bays — any truck line loads at any bay; the garage is a depot, so buy trucks right there
  • A railhead — trains, ships and trucks at any station in reach load from it and unload into it, like any warehouse
  • Harbours too — beside a port it stores what the ships bring in, and the tools and machines the oil platforms and fishing grounds want
  • The Storage split — slide space between cargos of one kind, lock the ones you want kept. Space moves; stock never gets thrown away
  • Porters until 1925 — a man with a sack barrow works the sheds; on New Year 1925 the forklifts arrive, a third faster at the bays. Your stock stays put

How to start

  1. Warehouses menu > the PYT tab > Factory Buffer
  2. Pick the Store size and whether it should make the Buffer line and buy the trucks
  3. Drop it onto the road beside a factory or farm (within about 400 m). A few seconds later its stores stand behind the office, and the first trucks leave the garage

Mod developer's tip: keep the train out of reach of the factory, so it always keeps the produce fresh! With the station reaching the buffer but not the factory, everything flows farm → trucks → buffer → train, in order.

Big brother, little brother

Already running PYT - Mega Warehouse? That's the big one: a locked store for every cargo in the game, 500 to 5,000 each, with built-in truck stops since 1.3. Factory Buffer is for one factory at a time. They work side by side, and a buffer feeds a Mega Warehouse by train as happily as it feeds a factory.

Worum es geht: Setzt den Fabrikpuffer neben eine Fabrik oder einen Hof. Er liest, was die Fabrik verbraucht und herstellt, und baut für jede Ware ein eigenes festes Lager – die Wolle eines Hofs blockiert nie wieder sein Fleisch. Latest update

1.1.7 — ON THE CLOCK

The buffer's bills stopped when the calendar did. They don't any more.

In plain terms: the running cost now ticks with play time, like the game's own upkeep, the same fix as Mega Warehouse 2.4.1.

  • Fixed: the running cost is booked once every game month of play time, like the game's own upkeep — a paused or slowed calendar no longer pauses or slows the bill
  • Fixed: the 1.1.6 tip about switching cargos off was wrong — an off cargo hands its space to the others, so the bill follows the buffer's size and how full it is
  • Thanks: to JamesJustice, who found it on Mega Warehouse
