2.4.1ON THE CLOCK

JamesJustice paused the calendar and the warehouse stopped sending bills. The accountant has been spoken to.

In plain terms: the running cost now ticks with play time, like the game's own upkeep, so pausing or slowing the calendar no longer pauses or slows it.

  • Fixed: the running cost is booked once every game month of play time (about 2 minutes at normal speed), the way the game charges its own upkeep — a paused or slowed calendar no longer pauses or slows the bill. Pausing the game itself still stops it, as it stops everything
  • Fixed: the 2.4 tip about switching cargos off was wrong — an off cargo hands its space to the others of its kind, so the camp's capacity and its bill stay the same. The bill follows the camp's size and how full it is right now
  • Thanks: to JamesJustice for testing it properly

Neu in 2.4.1: Die Betriebskosten laufen jetzt mit der Spielzeit, wie der Unterhalt des Spiels – ein angehaltener oder langsamer Kalender hält die Rechnung nicht mehr an.

  • Behoben: die Betriebskosten werden einmal je Spielmonat Spielzeit gebucht (etwa 2 Minuten bei normaler Geschwindigkeit), so wie das Spiel seinen Unterhalt verlangt – ein angehaltener oder langsamer Kalender hält die Rechnung nicht mehr an. Pausiert man das Spiel selbst, steht sie still, wie alles andere
  • Behoben: der Tipp aus 2.4 zum Abschalten von Frachten war falsch – eine abgeschaltete Fracht gibt ihren Platz an die anderen ihrer Art, Kapazität und Rechnung bleiben gleich. Die Rechnung folgt der Größe des Lagers und wie voll es gerade ist
  • Danke: an JamesJustice für das gründliche Testen
2.4YOUR GAME, YOUR RULES

JamesJustice told us a Mega Warehouse was so cheap it felt like cheating. He was right. Now you choose how much it hurts.

In plain terms: three new settings in the mod setup let hardcore players make warehouses expensive to build and to run; left alone, nothing changes.

  • New: three settings in the mod setup, before a game loads — Build cost (the game's price, or 2, 3, 5 or 10 times it: the build menu, placing, Grow and Upgrade all charge it), Running cost (the game's upkeep, or $25k to $300k a year per 500 of capacity per cargo, replacing it — booked once a game month under Road maintenance) and Extra cost when full (0 to 200% more for a full camp)
  • New: the sliders show the prices for a 500 camp as you move them, with a short chart underneath; each warehouse's window shows its own running cost and a Size / Buy / Min / Max chart for your settings
  • Tip: the running cost follows the camp's size (its whole capacity) and, with the extra-when-full setting, how full it is right now — a lean camp pays less each month, and a smaller camp costs less
  • Left at Game / Game / 0%, the warehouse costs exactly what it did
  • Better: the alternative-bays keeper waits for a rebuild to finish before it looks at the lines
  • Thanks: to JamesJustice for the idea

Neu in 2.4: Drei neue Einstellungen im Mod-Setup machen Lager für Hardcore-Spieler teuer im Bau und im Betrieb; unverändert bleibt alles wie bisher.

  • Neu: drei Einstellungen im Mod-Setup, vor dem Laden eines Spiels – Baukosten (der Preis des Spiels oder das 2-, 3-, 5- oder 10-fache: Baumenü, Bauen, Vergrößern und Upgrade verlangen ihn), Betriebskosten (der Unterhalt des Spiels oder 25.000 bis 300.000 $ im Jahr je 500 Kapazität und Fracht, statt des Unterhalts – einmal im Spielmonat unter Straße/Unterhalt gebucht) und Aufschlag, wenn voll (0 bis 200 % mehr für ein volles Lager)
  • Neu: die Regler zeigen beim Verschieben die Preise für ein 500er-Lager, darunter eine kurze Tabelle; jedes Lagerfenster zeigt seine eigenen Betriebskosten und eine Tabelle Größe / Kauf / Min / Max für deine Einstellungen
  • Tipp: die Betriebskosten folgen der Größe des Lagers (seiner ganzen Kapazität) und, mit dem Aufschlag, wie voll es gerade ist – ein schlankes Lager zahlt jeden Monat weniger, ein kleineres kostet weniger
  • Bei Spiel / Spiel / 0 % kostet das Lager genau so viel wie bisher
  • Besser: der Buchten-Verwalter wartet, bis ein Umbau fertig ist, bevor er die Linien ansieht
  • Danke: an JamesJustice für die Idee

2.3.3QUICK ON ITS FEET

A player clicked his 1900 camp and watched his frame rate fall from 60 to 17. The camp had been counting every crate, sixty times a second. Now it counts them when a truck actually brings one or takes one away.

In plain terms: the warehouse window no longer slows the game, the alternative bays stay as you set them (one switch per warehouse decides whether they're handed out by themselves), tram rails survive an Upgrade, and the 1900 camp's yard gets carts, crates and barrels.

  • Fixed: opening a warehouse's window cost a lot of frame rate (60 down to 17 fps on a big 1900 camp) — the stores were re-read every frame; now only when a truck loads or unloads, and every 10 seconds for crates going off
  • Fixed: alternative bays came back after you switched them off — a check in the background put them back after every reload. It's gone: a stop gets every bay only when you add it or make a new line, and the new bays after an Upgrade. Your “don't use” stays as you set it
  • New: Auto-assign alternative bays in the warehouse's window, one switch per warehouse, kept in your save — on: every line stopping there may use every truck bay, new lines and the new bays after an Upgrade too; off: every line keeps its own bay and nothing changes what you set in the line window
  • Fixed: with the camp's own Tram track option, the yard's rails and wires vanished after an Upgrade (the trams still drove) — they stay now
  • New: the 1900 camp's yard is less bare — horse-less carts (flat, hay and water), crates, barrels and a water trough stand about in the free spots
  • Thanks: to Stephen Spry for both reports

2.1THE MRHAMSTRING SYSTEM

A player told us he couldn’t stop his warehouse taking a cargo. He was right — the lowest a cargo went was 50. So now there’s an Off switch, and it’s polite about what’s already on the shelves.

In plain terms: switch off any cargo you don’t want stored; its space goes to the others of its kind.

  • Click a cargo’s icon in the Storage split to switch it off: it isn’t stored here any more, its icon goes grey and locks, and its space is shared by the other cargos of its kind. Click again to switch it back on
  • Holding stock? You choose: Off once shipped out (nothing more comes in, the space frees up as it leaves, off when empty) or Destroy (gone at once, space freed now)
  • All off / All on for a whole kind — every liquid off in one click, say
  • Sliding a cargo right down to 0 switches it off too; Balance leaves switched-off cargos alone
  • Thanks: to MrHamstring, who asked for exactly this
2.0DRIVE RIGHT THROUGH

Watch the 1960 shed in action — one minute, real in-game footage. How to upgrade — 30 seconds, step by step.

It’s 1960. Your trucks are bigger, your towns are busier, and nobody has time to reverse into a loading bay any more. Meet the 1960 drive-through shed: trucks drive in one end, load along the docks and drive straight out the other.

  • The 1960 shed has its own button in the PYT warehouses tab, from 1960 on. The same locked store for every cargo — tanks, bins, racks with a travelling crane, a cold store, a gas station and its own truck depot.
  • Truck lanes: 4, 6, 8 or 12. Each lane is one stop, just like the game’s own truck station: the first truck drives to the front, the next pull up behind it, and they all load at once. Every lane is added to your lines by itself.
  • Real staff. People from your towns come to work here — 2 to 16 jobs by size. No staff: loading 3x. Fully staffed: 5x. The warehouse window shows who’s at work.
  • A bus stop for the staff in front of the office — buses only, passengers only. Trucks never use it.
  • Lit for the night shift. Light masts fill every dark corner of the yard.
  • Upgrade a camp into the shed from 1960 — same stores, same stock, same split. The upgrade can now add truck bays and lanes too.
  • A bigger garage: the truck depot looks after 12 to 24 trucks at a time (was 8).
  • Your language on the sign: the office board shows your company’s name and “Warehouse” in your game’s language.
  • The 1900 camp got a polish: a dirt yard, the whole truck yard cobbled from gate to gate with stone kerbs, a glass roof on the rack hall, brick gables and a crane sized to its yard.
  • An easter egg: build the 12-lane shed and take a close look through the office window.

🙏 Thanks to Sascha ( P4NtR / @ZoneOffGames) for showing Mega Warehouse on YouTube — his “only four truck stops” started the bigger truck offices, and the 12-lane office is his.

1.3A MEGA UPGRADE!

Mega Warehouse has had a mega upgrade!

First, let's get something straight. This isn't a bug-fix update.

We fumigated the warehouse before opening day and made sure there weren't any bugs hiding between the crates.

But after such an overwhelmingly positive launch — 100% thumbs up! — we thought, why stop at making a warehouse that works?

Let's bring it up to the PYT standard and give it a little of that magic some of you have come to expect from us.

So, as a thank-you to everyone who's supported our little mod, we've been busy.

Blueprint Exchange and the PYT mods are fan-made community projects for Transport Fever 2 and Transport Fever 3 players. It is unofficial and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or the responsibility of the game’s developers or publishers.
