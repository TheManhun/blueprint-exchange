What it does

Two sweeping white shells over your highway. Traffic splits into its own booth lanes — buses and trucks in the red lane at the kerb, cars beside them — slows through the booths and joins up again. From 1990.

Works on its own: no other mods needed. Add Pay Your Tolls and the Wave starts earning.

HOW TO START (1990 or later)

  1. Road menu > the PYT tab
  2. Drop the plaza or a Wave Gantry onto your highway — it fits itself
  3. Add the signs either side: Both puts THANK YOU across the road

On its own — a scenery pack that places itself

  • Harbour City Toll Plaza — one-way or two-way, 1 to 3 lanes; it fits itself to your road, with the town it serves on its floodlit board
  • Trams welcome — on a road with tram track, the plaza lays its own through the bus and truck booth
  • Wave Gantry — drop it on any road: it picks its own size from the lanes, centres itself, faces one-way traffic, and puts the town's name on its board. Widen the road later and it follows
  • Harbour City signs — HARBOUR CITY TOLL 1 km and THANK YOU. Entry, Exit or Both: they turn to their traffic and stand at the road's edge by themselves
  • Wave street lamps — single or twin, original, warm yellow or white, low to high
  • Chevrons — lit amber at night, on the way in
  • Barriers — white rail walls of 4 to 20 m and LED noses, on the plaza's barrier spots or anywhere you like
  • No Entry signs — lit, at the plaza's exits

Click any piece and choose Configure to swap its style in place, using the bar along the bottom. Everything is in the Road menu's PYT tab — one tab for every PYT pack you have.

With Pay Your Tolls — the wave earns

  • Every car, bus and truck through the booths pays into your Finances
  • Your speed — 10, 20 or 30 km/h through the booths, or Off, from the toll's card
  • Toll keepers — workers from the nearest town staff the booths: +10% or +25% on the takings
  • The Wave joins PYT's own buttons — a Toll Gantry era, a Toll Plaza style, an Entry / Exit Sign era and a Lights style — so your build menu stays tidy

Latest update

2.0 — MIRROR, MIRROR

Mirror, mirror on the motorway, who drives on the proper side today? Both of you, finally.

And while you're there: have a look through the booth windows. 😏

  • In plain terms: with the Left Hand Traffic mod, the whole Wave plaza is built mirrored for left-hand traffic — islands, booths, lane lights and the split before the booths — and every board still reads the right way round
  • Right the moment you place it: the plaza and its tram track sort themselves out within seconds
  • The booths: see-through windows and a furnished interior
  • With Pay Your Tolls: one toll keeper per booth — a full crew earns +25% on the takings; a bigger plaza may need a minute to fill up after the update
  • Your existing plazas update themselves: no bulldozing
