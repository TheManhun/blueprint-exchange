PYT - Road Train Mixed Cargo Trucks

Seven long-haul carriers that take FOUR kinds of cargo at once: an 1895 horse-drawn parcels train, a 1914 semi, a 1936 streamliner, a 1955 road train and three B-doubles up to the 2025 electric. Up to 216 cargo in six mixed slots.

What it does

Grain, fuel, tools and planks — up to 216 of them, in one truck, on one trip. Seven long-haul carriers: a horse-drawn parcels train, semis, a road train and B-doubles, and every cargo slot takes bulk, liquid, goods or flatbed cargo.

Version 1.0

The game's trucks carry one kind of cargo at a time. A town that wants four things means four lines — or one long truck that brings the lot. These pull one or two trailers, load what's waiting, up to four kinds at once, and drop each one where it's wanted.

What you get

  • Seven long-haul carriers from 1895, with four or six mixed-cargo slots.
  • Your company name on the trailers.
  • 12 languages.
  • The Euro and electric B-doubles share their cabs with the single-trailer trucks in PYT - Late Mixed Cargo Trucks.
  • Smaller single-trailer trucks: PYT - Early Mixed Cargo Trucks (1890–1985) and PYT - Late Mixed Cargo Trucks (1975–2025).

The trucks

  • PYT Parcels Train (1895–1925) — before the lorry: four draft horses and two vans, a carrier’s train with carriage lamps for the night. 6 slots, 36 cargo, 17 km/h.
  • PYT Mixed Cargo Semi (1914–1935) — where it all began: a light truck pulling one of the first semi-trailers. 4 slots, 15 cargo, 20 km/h. Slow, but it carries more than any lorry of its day.
  • PYT Mixed Cargo Streamliner (1936–1960) — a streamlined 1930s semi, its riveted van carrying your company name in big letters. 4 slots, 24 cargo, 50 km/h.
  • PYT Mixed Cargo Road Train (1955–1990) — a bonneted outback prime mover and two canvas-tilt trailers. 6 slots, 120 cargo, 55 km/h.
  • PYT Mixed Cargo B-Double (from 1990) — an Australian B-double: a bonneted prime mover and two curtain-siders. 6 slots, 216 cargo, 100 km/h.
  • PYT Mixed Cargo Euro B-Double (from 2010) — a modern European high-roof tractor and two curtain-siders. 6 slots, 216 cargo, 100 km/h.
  • PYT Mixed Cargo E-B-Double (from 2025) — an electric long-nose tractor and two curtain-siders, quiet and clean. 6 slots, 216 cargo, 100 km/h.

How it works

  • Every slot takes bulk, liquid, goods or flatbed cargo — the truck's window shows all four specializations.
  • Set the mix on each stop's Load panel — the game's own panel. Pick the cargos, give each a share, and the truck fills its slots to match.
  • Flexible, but slower to load: mixed trucks load at the universal rate, slower than a specialist truck.
  • Worth buying as a plain truck too: fair, vanilla-style speed, power and capacity for its era. The mixed load is the bonus.
  • Working indicators (trafficators on the old ones) and night lights; the trailers follow the road the way the game's own semis do.
