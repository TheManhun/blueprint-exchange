Report a Problem

Something didn’t work? Send us what happened, the log and the blueprint — they tell us exactly why.

Before you send

  1. Make the problem happen once more. If the game crashed, don’t start it again yet — it writes a new log every time it starts.
  2. Open File Explorer and go to C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\userdata, then your number folder, then 3493540 → local → crash_dump.
  3. Attach stdout.txt from there. It has everything we need, including your list of mods.
  1. Do it again in the game, until the problem shows (for example “This spot won’t take it”). Then stop — the log keeps only the last few hundred lines.
  2. Open your Transport Fever 2 folder (Steam → right-click Transport Fever 2 → Manage → Browse local files). The files are next to TransportFever2.exe.
  3. Attach blueprint_exchange_log.txt, and if it is about one of your own blueprints, its blueprint_<name>.lua.

Your report

Your private edit key is removed from the file before it is sent — nobody but you can ever update your blueprint.

Only plain text is accepted (.txt and .lua, nothing that can run). Reports are private: only the BPX team can read them. Prefer to talk it through? The community forum is open too.

Blueprint Exchange is a fan-made community project for Transport Fever 2 players. It is unofficial and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or the responsibility of the game’s developers or publishers.

Inspired by Copy It! by okeating — a superb TF2 utility whose approach to capturing and rebuilding constructions and track is the foundation Blueprint Exchange builds on.
