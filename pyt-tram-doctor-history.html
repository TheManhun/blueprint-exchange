1.0.3THE RIGHT TOOL

The grey road tool button looked like a button and acted like a sticker. Now it does something: it flies you to the road piece the doctor can't fix itself and tells you what to build there — click again for the next piece. And Tram Doctor on its own now has its PYT — Settings (language, the PYT button fade).

Neu in 1.0.3: Der Knopf „Straßenwerkzeug“ fliegt jetzt zum Straßenstück und sagt, was dort zu bauen ist; PYT — Einstellungen (Sprache, Ausblenden) auch ohne andere PYT-Mods.

1.0.2QUIET PLEASE

Our NEW button bounced like a puppy at the door, shoving every button beside it. It has been rehomed. And clicking the PYT row could pull the rug out from under the game — the rug is now nailed down.

In plain terms: clicking the PYT row no longer crashes the game, the NEWS button is gone, and PYT — Settings no longer stutters on big maps.

  • Fixed: on big saves the game could freeze for about a second every 10 seconds, even with no window open — the same fix as PYT - Minimap 1.4.1
  • Fixed: clicking the PYT icon or a tile in its row could crash the game — the button was taken away while the game was still handling the click (seen together with the Mod Bar mod)
  • Gone: the NEWS button in the PYT row. It bounced and pushed other buttons around, and you told us you didn't want it — we listen
  • Tidier: the PYT row shows one settings cog (some setups showed two), and the 'fade until the mouse is over it' setting is always in PYT — Settings
  • Smoother: with PYT — Settings open on a big map, its performance meter no longer stalls the game every 10 seconds (it could make the sound flutter) — it reads once when you open it, then every 2 minutes
  • Thanks: to QuickNiq for the crash log and honest feedback, and to the Mod Bar players who spoke up

Neu in 1.0.2: Ein Klick in die PYT-Leiste lässt das Spiel nicht mehr abstürzen, der NEWS-Knopf ist weg, und PYT — Settings ruckelt auf großen Karten nicht mehr.

1.0.1TACK! ДЯКУЮ!

The doctor has been learning languages. Two more, to be exact — for some very important people in Epod’s house.

  • In plain terms: Tram Doctor now speaks Swedish and Ukrainian, and follows the PYT Language setting like the rest of the family (Auto still uses the game’s language)
  • Never a cargo bay: a station bay that handles cargo is no longer picked for buses or trams when the stop has a passenger bay — and a line already on one is flagged, with Fix moving it (thanks Epod, who had to move one by hand)
  • Station bays to spare: at a station building the line gets the station's other fitting bays as alternatives, so trams and buses use any free one — and cargo trams get cargo bays
  • PYT — Settings: the PYT button can fade until your mouse is over it
  • Something reads oddly? Tell us in the comments — we fix translations fast
