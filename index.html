Blueprint Exchange and the PYT mods are fan-made community projects for Transport Fever 2 and Transport Fever 3 players. It is unofficial and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or the responsibility of the game’s developers or publishers.

Inspired by Copy It! by okeating — a superb TF2 utility whose approach to capturing and rebuilding constructions and track is the foundation Blueprint Exchange builds on.

Downloaded

  1. Move into your game folder — usually C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Transport Fever 2 (Steam → right-click Transport Fever 2 → Manage → Browse local files opens it). Downloading more? Each one gets its own file name, so grab a few before moving them in. Or make a one-click importer that does the moving for you.
  2. In game: BPX → Import downloads. It lands in your library as — one press picks up everything you moved in, not just this one.
  3. Place, aim, click. Need more help? See the download and import guide.

Tip: the Blueprint Manager (Chrome / Edge) can put downloads straight into your game folder — no moving files.

You’ve downloaded 10 blueprints

That fills all 10 download names the mod knows (blueprint_import.lua to blueprint_import_10.lua). Before you download any more:

  1. Move all 10 files from your Downloads folder into your Transport Fever 2 game folder (the one with TransportFever2.exe) — or run your one-click importer.
  2. In game: BPX → Import downloads. All 10 land in your library in one press.

Please do this first. The next downloads start again at blueprint_import.lua. If the first 10 haven’t been moved in and imported, the new files will overwrite them — and those blueprints will be lost.
