What it does

Drop it beside any road and it shows that road's real speed limit — and changes by itself when you upgrade the road. km/h, UK mph or the American SPEED LIMIT sign.

PYT - Speed Signs. Pace Your Traffic. A PYT (Play Your Transport) mod by Epod. A sister of PYT - Town Signs and PYT - Distance Signs.

Choose Auto and the sign looks at the road beside it: a 50 km/h street gets a 50, a country road an 80, a motorway a 130. Upgrade the road and every Auto sign along it follows within seconds — no more signs that lie.

Deutsch? Eine Kurzfassung auf Deutsch findet ihr weiter unten.

What you get

  • Auto reads the road — the sign shows the road's own speed limit, rounded to the nearest real sign, and keeps up when the road changes
  • Your units, your style — km/h or mph (or follow the game's setting), International round signs or the US SPEED LIMIT plate
  • Pick any sign — 30-130 km/h, 20-70 mph or US 15-80 mph from one list, or No limit for the national speed limit sign
  • Snap to road — it stands at the kerb, facing the drivers on its side. Free — put it exactly where you like (Auto still reads the road)
  • Your language — the build menu speaks all 13 Transport Fever 3 languages
  • Scenery only — charges nothing, slows nothing, needs no other mod

How to start

  1. Road menu > the PYT tab > Speed Limit Sign
  2. Leave Speed: Auto and Snap to road
  3. Drop it beside a road, on the side of the drivers who should read it — it faces them and shows their limit
