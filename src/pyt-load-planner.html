What it does

No more clicking + on every cargo, every box at 100%, and trying to remember how much room is left. Open any freight line: its stops show what they load, and a click lets you share the vehicle out with sliders — it saves itself.

▶ Get the whole set in one click: PYT - Mixed Cargo Collection — the Early and Late trucks, the Vanilla Conversion Kit (59 base-game vehicles) and Load Planner.

Version 1.1.1

How it works

  • Open a freight line (or a vehicle, or the PYT page): every stop shows what it loads.
  • Click a stop: click cargo icons to switch them on, then a slider per cargo — moving one moves the others, the vehicle always adds up. The padlock keeps one where it is; Balance splits evenly.
  • Empty has its own slider: the room the vehicle leaves the stop with.
  • It saves itself, using the game's own stop settings — a second after your last change.
  • Trains, ships and aircraft step in whole wagons and holds: a section per kind of wagon, and the general wagons with their own sliders.

Smart fill & bin expired

  • Smart fill (per stop): your plan is the aim, but as each vehicle pulls in, a cargo short in stock gives its room to cargoes that have plenty — what the drop-off wants first. Nothing in stock? Your plan stays exactly as you set it.
  • Bin expired (per stop): clears cargo that has run out of delivery time from the warehouses and outputs the stop reaches, so vehicles load fresh.

Works with

  • Any freight vehicle, base game or modded — trucks, trains, ships, planes, helicopters.
  • Mixed cargo trucks: PYT - Early Mixed Cargo Trucks, PYT - Late Mixed Cargo Trucks, or the PYT - Mixed Cargo Vanilla Conversion Kit.
  • Using Compartments for Vanilla vehicle (by Maik_Chaos)? Load Manager plans its mixed planes, ships and helicopters too — every compartment a slider, right in the line window.
  • 14 languages, following the PYT Language setting (or the game's).
