1.1THE LEFT-HAND DRIVERS WIN

Not every sign in the picture comes with this mod — they are three separate PYT mods: PYT - Speed Signs (this mod) · PYT - Distance Signs · PYT - Town Signs — or get all three at once: PYT - Complete Road Signs Collection

OK, the left-hand drivers win — even if I am one, being in Australia. Every sign now knows which side of the road you drive on.

  • Left-hand traffic — with the Left Hand Traffic mod, a sign snapped to a road now faces the traffic driving on the left, all by itself
  • New setting: Traffic drives on — Auto / Right / Left. Auto follows your game (left with the Left Hand Traffic mod); Right or Left if you ever need to set it yourself — on a placed sign too
  • In plain terms: signs face the right way on left-hand maps; right-hand maps don't change

★ Thank you, NovaOfZion and jimbo_1977! You asked for left-hand driving — here it is.
