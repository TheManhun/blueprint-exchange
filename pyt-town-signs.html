What it does

WELCOME TO your town — by name, with its population — and SEE YOU SOON on the way out. Every era from 1900 to 2020, and it positions itself beside the road.

Drop a Town Sign beside any road into town. It reads the nearest town and prints it on the plate: WELCOME TO BRIGHTLINGSEA, POP. 423. As the town grows, the number grows with it.

What you get

  • The town's real name — read from the nearest town, no typing
  • Its population — kept up to date as the town grows (Show / Hide)
  • Every era — 1900 timber and stone, 1940 enamel, 1980 highway green, 2020 a lit monument sign. Auto picks the style of the year
  • Welcome, See you soon or Both — Both places the pair: WELCOME where you drop it, SEE YOU SOON straight across the road for the drivers leaving
  • Snap to road — it stands at the kerb and turns to face its drivers by itself. Free — put it exactly where you like, it still reads the town
  • Scenery only — charges nothing, slows nothing, needs no other mod

How to start

  1. Road menu > the PYT tab > Town Sign: Welcome / See you soon
  2. Pick Both and Snap to road
  3. Drop it beside a road into town, on the right of the drivers coming in — the See you soon appears across the road

Latest update

1.2.1 — NOBODY REPAINTS A SIGN EVERY MINUTE

In the real world the population changes by the minute, and nobody climbs the ladder to repaint the sign. Ours did. Every minute. Every sign. All at once.

In plain terms: a performance fix -- the signs now check their town in turn, about every 10 minutes each, and round the figure like a real sign.

  • Fixed: the signs re-read their town every minute and rebuilt on every population tick, all signs in the same frame -- on a big map that was a stutter every minute
  • Now: each sign is checked about every 10 minutes, the signs taken in turn at an even pace (30 signs = one every 20 seconds), and the population is rounded like a real sign (to 10, 50 or 100)
  • Unchanged: a new sign is filled the moment you place it
  • Thanks: to thibaulthc, who found and reported it — reports like this improve the mods for everyone, so keep them coming
