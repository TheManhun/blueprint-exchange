What it does

The next towns ahead, by real road distance — the capital crowned on its line — in your own km or miles. Every era from 1900 to 2020, and it fills itself.

Drop a Distance Sign beside a road. It follows the road the way its drivers are going — through every junction, left and right — and lists the towns they will reach, nearest first: Thirsk 3, Brightlingsea 9, Epworth 11. The numbers are real road distances, not as the crow flies.

💡 Good to know: a sign shows the 5 biggest towns its road leads to (the capital always, when it's ahead), listed nearest first — so a small town in between can be left out on purpose. Rather have the 5 nearest towns down the road? Switch it under the PYT button > Settings > Signs (since 1.2).

What you get

  • Only what is down THIS road — a dead end to one village lists just that village; a road through a junction lists the towns down every branch
  • Up to five towns — the biggest when more lie ahead, or the nearest (your pick in PYT – Settings > Signs), nearest at the top; the board shrinks to fit when fewer do
  • The capital, crowned — the town of your headquarters gets a crown on its line whenever the road leads there
  • Your units — kilometres or miles, from your game settings
  • It keeps itself up to date — build a road to a new town and the sign adds it within seconds; remove it and the town goes
  • It moves out of the way — when a town grows over a sign placed on a country road, the sign walks out to 100 m past the new edge of town
  • Every era — 1900 painted timber, 1940 enamel, 1980 highway green (lit at night), 2020 motorway blue on a gantry with an LED bar. Auto picks the style of the year
  • Snap to road — it stands at the kerb facing the drivers on its side. Free — put it where you like
  • Scenery only — charges nothing, slows nothing, needs no other mod

How to start

  1. Road menu > the PYT tab > Distance Sign (beside the Town Sign)
  2. Leave Snap to road and Era: Auto
  3. Drop it beside a road, on the side of the drivers who should read it — it faces them and fills itself in a moment

Latest update

1.3.1 — QUIET PLEASE

Our NEW button bounced like a puppy at the door, shoving every button beside it. It has been rehomed. And clicking the PYT row could pull the rug out from under the game — the rug is now nailed down.

In plain terms: clicking the PYT row no longer crashes the game, the NEWS button is gone, and PYT — Settings no longer stutters on big maps.

  • Fixed: on big saves the game could freeze for about a second every 10 seconds, even with no window open — the same fix as PYT - Minimap 1.4.1
  • Fixed: clicking the PYT icon or a tile in its row could crash the game — the button was taken away while the game was still handling the click (seen together with the Mod Bar mod)
  • Gone: the NEWS button in the PYT row. It bounced and pushed other buttons around, and you told us you didn't want it — we listen
  • Tidier: the PYT row shows one settings cog (some setups showed two), and the 'fade until the mouse is over it' setting is always in PYT — Settings
  • Smoother: with PYT — Settings open on a big map, its performance meter no longer stalls the game every 10 seconds (it could make the sound flutter) — it reads once when you open it, then every 2 minutes
  • Thanks: to QuickNiq for the crash log and honest feedback, and to the Mod Bar players who spoke up
